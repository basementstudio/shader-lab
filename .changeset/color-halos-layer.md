Add a Color Halos effect layer: soft multicolor halos that follow dark shapes, light shapes or cutouts, blurred smoothly and mapped through an editable ramp, with Threshold, Spread, Intensity, Fade, contour Bands, Keep Shape to draw the crisp original on top, and cross-shaped flares from small bright points. Four starting styles (Gradient Maps, Aura, Cross Flare, Contour Bands). Halos extend past a shape's edge, so they work on text and transparent images inside groups. It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
