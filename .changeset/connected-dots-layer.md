Add a Connected Dots effect layer: the image becomes jittered points linked to their neighbors. Graph links dots by tone with links that thicken in the darks; Blobs melts dots and links into organic masses; Plexus draws thin lines that fade with distance. Circle, square, plus or ring dots sized by tone, a tone cutoff that drops the background, palette-by-tone (flat bands from an editable ramp of up to 8 stops), source or ink color, a color, image or transparent background, and Drift so links form and break over time. Four starting styles (Portrait Graph, Ink Blobs, Plexus, Constellation).
