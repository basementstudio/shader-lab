Add an Erosion effect layer that crumbles the image into speckle along its edges, light or dark tones, or the border of a cutout, with Erode, Edge Width, Speckle Size, Clumping and Scatter (fragments thrown across the edge), revealing paper or transparent holes, frozen or animated. Three starting styles (Disintegrate, Paper Erosion, Crumbled Cutout). It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
