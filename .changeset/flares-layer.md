Add a Flares effect layer: light flares from the brightest small points in the image, with Threshold, Isolation (large bright areas stay clean), Intensity, 1 to 16 rays, rotation, length, secondary ray length, length jitter, thickness, falloff, ray and core colors and a core glow. Five starting styles (Cross, Star, Starburst, Asterisk, Anamorphic). Flares extend over transparent areas. It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
