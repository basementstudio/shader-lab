Add a Blur effect layer built on a seven-level blur pyramid with B-spline sampling, so any radius stays smooth and the blur size can change per pixel. Blur From: Uniform, Depth (depth of field from the scene depth map), Linear and Radial focus zones, or Luminance, with Focus, Focus Range, Transition, Center, Band Angle and Invert Focus. Kinds: Gaussian, Lens bokeh with Highlights, and Motion. Grain keeps large blurs from banding. Five starting styles (Depth of Field, Tilt-Shift, Vignette, Soft, Motion). Directional Blur, Progressive Blur and Fluted Glass leave the add-layer menu; saved projects keep rendering them.
