Add a Glass effect layer that replaces Fluted Glass: reeded (round or sharp flutes), hammered, pyramid, hex or frosted glass where every cell is a small lens, the scene blurs with its distance behind the glass (uniform or from the depth map, through the shared blur pyramid), and thin rims, grooves, frost, dispersion and tint make it read as glass. Six starting styles (Reeded, Frosted Reeded, Hammered, Pyramid, Hex, Frosted). The Blur layer now shares the same blur pyramid module.
