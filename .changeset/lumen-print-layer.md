Add a Lumen Print effect layer: an analog photographic print with sun-print toning through an editable palette, solarization with edge lines, diffusion and halation, highlights washed into paper, burned borders and grain. Six starting styles (Lumen, Cyanotype, Burned, Sabattier, Washed, Eroded). It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
