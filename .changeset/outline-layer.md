Add an Outline effect layer on a jump-flood distance field: solid, double, dashed or scalloped cloud outlines around text, cutouts or dark and light shapes, with offset (negative draws inside), width, repeated rings, fill between shape and outline, and a line-only mode. Six starting styles (Stroke, Cloud, Sticker, Contour Rings, Cut Line, Double).
