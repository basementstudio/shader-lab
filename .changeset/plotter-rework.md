Rework Plotter into a pen-plotter drawing: Hatch (crosshatch layers by tone), Flow (dense lines following the forms), Contour (smooth tone contours), Squiggle (rows that wave in the darks), Spiral (one continuous spiral whose line thickens with tone) and Stipple (dots by density), with pen width, pressure, wobble, ink bleed, ink, source or three pens, paper color, grain or transparent paper, and seven starting styles. The original parameters and their defaults are kept, so saved plotter layers still open.
