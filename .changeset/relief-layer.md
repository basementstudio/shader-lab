Add a Relief effect layer that embosses or debosses the image into a lit surface, with height from luminance, scene depth or a cutout's alpha, Depth, Bevel, light angle, elevation and ambient, a single material or the source colors with specular highlights, grain, and optional parallel or radial engraving. Five starting styles (Silver Plate, Letterpress, Blind Emboss, Gold Foil, Source Relief). It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
