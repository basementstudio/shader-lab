Displaced Rings gains ring shapes (Circle, Triangle, Square, Polygon with Sides) alongside the Full/Half cut, per-band Rotation Jitter and Width Jitter driven by Seed, and Lines: thin strokes on the band boundaries that can keep repeating across the canvas beyond the outer band, with width, color and opacity. New controls default to off, so existing layers and saved projects look the same.
