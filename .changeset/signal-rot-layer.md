Add a Signal Rot effect layer for scanner drag and decaying signal: held streaks, snaking wobble, uneven scan speed, torn bands with paper dropouts, chroma drift, crushed levels and line noise, frozen or animated. Four starting styles (Scanner Drag, Signal Rot, Tape Wobble, Torn Scan). It renders identically in the editor, exports and the runtime package.
